## Key points
- Revision episode: interview questions on **images, fonts and third-party scripts**.
- Know why `sizes` matters, the slow-hero checklist, how to avoid both invisible text and font shifts, and how to add a chat widget safely.

## Interview answer
> For images I serve responsive, modern formats with srcset and sizes, make the hero discoverable with high priority and never lazy-load it. For fonts I self-host, subset and preload the critical one, with a metric-matched fallback. For third parties I measure their cost and load them late or behind facades, reserving space for anything they render.

## Quick quiz
<details><summary>Why does sizes matter on a responsive image?</summary>

Without it, the browser assumes the image is 100vw wide and picks a much larger file than needed.
</details>
<details><summary>Checklist for a slow hero image (LCP)?</summary>

It's an `<img>` in the HTML (not CSS/JS), has fetchpriority="high" or a preload, isn't lazy-loaded, is correctly sized via srcset/sizes, uses AVIF/WebP, and is served from a CDN.
</details>
<details><summary>How do you avoid both invisible text and font layout shift?</summary>

Use font-display swap with a size-adjusted fallback font (or optional), self-host and preload the key woff2 file, and subset it.
</details>
<details><summary>Marketing wants a chat widget. How do you add it without hurting performance?</summary>

Measure its cost, load it behind a facade or after first interaction/idle, reserve space for its button to avoid CLS, and keep it off the critical path. Re-check field INP and CLS after launch.
</details>
